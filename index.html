How Deep Does a Next-Concept Model Need to Run?

Measuring the Depth Ceiling of Coarse-to-Fine Text Generation

Next-concept models write sequences in a fixed ladder of K coarse-to-fine parallel passes. We formulate the oracle depth ceiling — the exact share of passes a rule with perfect foresight can skip at equal quality — and measure it across tasks (37.7% on XSum summarisation, replicating at 39.3% and 37.6% across cohorts). We discover the meter slope phenomenon (the last pass leaves length unchanged and replaces 1 in 7 content words) and build a training-free entity saturation stopping rule capturing one-third of the ceiling (saving 1 pass in 8) at strict content fidelity.
